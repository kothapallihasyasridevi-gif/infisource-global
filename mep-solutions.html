MEP Solutions Sourcing for Commercial and Industrial Projects

MEP procurement rarely fails for want of a specification — it fails because the electrical, lighting, plumbing and pumping lines are each bought from a different supplier, on a different timeline, with different paperwork. InfiSource Corporation Private Limited is a B2B corporate procurement and strategic sourcing partner for procurement heads, project engineers, MEP consultants, EPC contractors and facilities managers who need one accountable point of contact across the whole MEP bill instead of a chain of separate vendors. We source to your specification and your bill of quantities, and consolidate recurring and project-based MEP purchasing under a single partner.

What We Source

We cover the material lines that sit behind a building's mechanical, electrical and plumbing services. The range below is sourced to specification through a verified pan-India supplier network — we are a sourcing and supply partner, not a manufacturer.

Electrical, wiring and switchgear. Cables and wires, switches and sockets, MCBs and DB boxes, wiring devices, panels and switchgear, and conduits and cable trays. This is the distribution backbone of any project — the material that has to match a make, a rating and a standard before it reaches site, and the category where mismatched makes cause the most rework.

Lighting. LED lighting across the fittings a commercial or industrial facility runs — where lamp type, wattage and fitting selection are driven by the space being lit, not a catalogue default.

Plumbing and sanitary. Pipes and fittings, sanitary ware, valves, and water heaters and geysers. These lines are specified early but often procured late, so they are the ones most exposed to substitution; we hold them to the original make and specification.

Pumps and motors. Pumps and motors for the water, utility and process duties a facility or plant depends on, matched to the duty point and make your consultant has approved.

Who This Is For

We work with buyers whose MEP requirement is specified and scheduled:

  • Fit-out projects. Commercial interiors, offices and institutional fit-outs where the MEP package is bought against a consultant's specification and has to arrive in sequence with the civil and finishing trades.
  • Factory maintenance. Plants buying replacement cables, switchgear, lighting, valves and pumps against running maintenance schedules, where a repeat make matters more than a one-off price.
  • EPC material supply. Contractors who need MEP lines sourced against a BOQ and delivered phase-wise so the package tracks the project programme rather than a single drop.
  • Infrastructure sites. Distributed sites where the same specification has to be met across multiple locations and one sourcing partner removes the need to manage each site's suppliers separately.

Brands We Source Through

We source through established MEP brands — Polycab, Legrand, Schneider, Astral, Jaquar, Finolex, Havells, Anchor and Crompton — as multi-brand sourcing, not single-brand supply. That matters because a real BOQ almost never specifies one make throughout: the switchgear may call for one approved brand while the sanitary ware, cabling and pumps each call for another. Multi-brand sourcing lets us match each line to the make and specification named in your documents, and it gives you a substitute route when a specified make is unavailable, without the buyer having to open a new vendor relationship for each brand.

How We Handle MEP Requirements

  • We work from your BOQ. Share the bill of quantities, the material list or the specification and we quote against it, line by line, rather than against a generic catalogue.
  • We match make and specification. Every line is sourced to the brand, rating and standard your consultant or engineer has approved, so what arrives is what was specified.
  • Phase-wise delivery aligned to project schedules. We can structure supply to follow your programme — the lines needed for a given stage supplied for that stage — rather than pushing the whole package to site at once.
  • Approvals and documentation. We work within your approval process and provide the documentation your project requires alongside the material.
  • Warranty-backed branded material. Material is sourced as branded product, with the manufacturer's warranty attaching to what is supplied.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you supply against a BOQ with multiple specified brands?

Yes. Multi-brand sourcing is the basis of how we work in this category. A BOQ that names one make for switchgear, another for sanitary ware and a third for cabling is handled as a single requirement.

Do you supply the whole MEP package or individual lines?

Both. Some buyers hand us the complete package; others start with one line — often cabling and switchgear — and add others later. There is no requirement to take the full range.

Can you match the make my consultant has approved?

We source to the specification you provide, including the approved make. Where a specified make needs a substitute, that is raised with you for approval rather than decided unilaterally.

Can material be delivered in phases to follow our project programme?

Yes — phase-wise supply aligned to project schedules is part of how we handle MEP requirements. Share the programme and the stage-wise material list and we structure supply to it.

Is the material branded and warranty-backed?

Material is sourced as branded product through our supplier network, with the manufacturer's warranty applying to what is supplied.
