Corporate Gifting & Uniforms for Employee and Client Engagement

This page is for the HR head, admin head, procurement manager or facilities manager who has to arrange gifts, branded merchandise and staff uniforms across departments, sites and occasions — usually to a fixed per-head budget and a fixed date. The difficulty is rarely the product itself; it is coordinating the right brand, logo placement, sizes and quantity across several vendors at once. InfiSource Corporation Private Limited sources corporate gifting, uniforms and branded merchandise through a verified pan-India supplier network, so the whole requirement can be planned, specified and billed through one accountable partner.

What We Source

We source to specification rather than holding a fixed catalogue, so the range below is a starting point for a conversation. The groups reflect how these items are actually used.

Gifting and recognition. Gift Sets, Executive Gifting, Corporate Diaries, Drinkware & Bottles, Tech & Gadget Gifts, and Trophies & Awards. These are chosen for milestones — a festival, a service anniversary, a long-service award or a senior client relationship. Executive gifting and diaries are specified more carefully for finish and brand name, while gift sets and drinkware are usually chosen for volume and consistency across a large employee base.

Branded merchandise and apparel. Branded Merchandise, Uniforms & Apparel, Safety Uniforms, and Travel & Bags. Here the work is getting the branding and the fit right at scale — one logo treatment applied consistently across hundreds of pieces, and size sets that match the workforce. Safety uniforms are handled as a separate line because the specification and fabric differ from general staff apparel.

Onboarding and event kits. Onboarding Kits and Event & Conference Kits. A new joiner kit is assembled as a coordinated set — a diary, drinkware, a bag, apparel and a welcome item — packed and issued together. Event and conference kits are built the same way around a specific date and headcount, whether that is an annual day, a dealer meet or an off-site.

Who This Is For

The same category covers very different buying situations, and the planning differs for each:

  • Diwali and festive gifting. A large, fixed headcount, a hard date, and a per-head budget that has to stretch across the organisation without obvious inconsistency between grades.
  • Annual day and milestone events. Awards, trophies and gifts tied to an event date, often with named recipients and personalised items.
  • New joiner and onboarding kits. A recurring requirement as hiring continues, best set up as a repeatable kit rather than re-sourced each time.
  • Client thank-yous and executive gifting. Smaller quantities, closer attention to brand name and finish, and a choice that stays consistent across a relationship team.
  • Safety uniforms and site apparel. Plant, warehouse, facility and field staff, where the uniform has to suit the operating environment and be issued in the right size mix.

Brands We Source Through

InfiSource is a sourcing and supply partner, not a manufacturer. We source through established brands including Parker and Cross for writing instruments and executive gifting, Milton and Cello for drinkware and household gifting, American Tourister and Skybags for travel and bags, Wildcraft for outdoor and utility bags, and Van Heusen for formal apparel and uniforms.

Sourcing across several brands rather than one is the practical advantage. A single gifting programme often needs a premium writing instrument for senior recipients and a value option for the wider base, or a formal uniform for front-office staff and a rugged bag for field teams. Multi-brand sourcing lets each item be matched to the recipient group, instead of the whole programme being forced into one supplier's stock list.

How We Handle Gifting and Uniform Requirements

  • Working to a per-head budget. We start from the budget per person and the headcount, then build a specification that fits, rather than proposing items first. Where grade bands exist, the specification is set per band.
  • Brand and logo specification. We confirm the item and how the branding is applied — position, size and finish — before the requirement goes to the supplier network, so what is approved is what is sourced.
  • Bulk quantity planning. Quantities are planned against the actual distribution list, including spares where needed, so the count is settled before sourcing begins.
  • Size sets for uniforms. For apparel we plan a size-wise break-up rather than a flat quantity, and treat safety uniforms separately because their specification differs.
  • Festive and event timelines. Gifting and event requirements run to fixed dates. We work backwards from the occasion and flag where a decision is needed early, without promising a delivery date the supplier network cannot hold to.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you work to a fixed per-head budget?

Yes. Share the budget per person, the headcount and any grade bands, and we will build a specification that fits, rather than proposing items first.

Can the logo and branding be applied as we specify?

Yes. We confirm the branding position, size and finish at the specification stage, so what is agreed is what is sourced.

How do we handle sizes for uniforms?

We plan a size-wise break-up from your distribution list rather than a flat quantity of one size. Safety uniforms are specified separately from general staff apparel.

Can you supply across multiple offices or sites?

Yes. We work through a verified pan-India supplier network, so a requirement spanning more than one location is handled as a single programme.

Is this a single order or can it repeat?

Both. Recurring requirements such as onboarding kits can be set up as a repeatable specification, while festive and event orders are handled individually.
