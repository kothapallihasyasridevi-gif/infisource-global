Facility Maintenance Supplies for Factories and Commercial Sites

This page is for the plant head, maintenance manager, facilities manager or procurement manager accountable for keeping a site running and its spares stocked. The difficulty in maintenance buying is rarely one item; it is the steady flow of small, recurring, specification-sensitive purchases spread across many vendors, each with its own quotation and invoice. InfiSource sources professional-grade hardware, fasteners and consumables through a verified pan-India supplier network, so recurring and project-based maintenance purchasing comes through one accountable point of contact.

What We Source

Tools and cutting

Hand and power tools, cutting tools and measuring instruments — spanners, drills, grinders, saws, drill bits, taps and dies, and instruments used to check dimensions, alignment and tolerances. Items are sourced to the specification or part number you nominate.

Fasteners and adhesives

Fasteners, adhesives and sealants — bolts, nuts, screws, washers and anchors, with the adhesives, threadlockers and sealants used in assembly, repair and leak control. These high-frequency, low-value items quietly empty a stores bin and then stop a job.

Lubrication and abrasives

Lubricants, and abrasives — oils, greases and specialty lubricants for machinery, bearings and moving parts, and the abrasives used in grinding, cutting, finishing and surface preparation.

Bearings and spares

Bearings, and plumbing and hardware spares — rotating-component spares for pumps, motors and conveyors, with the plumbing and general hardware spares that keep plant services and buildings working.

Safety and site equipment

Ladders and scaffolding, paints and coatings, and maintenance consumables — access equipment for working at height, coatings for protection and touch-up work, and the everyday consumables (tapes, gloves, cleaning items) a store runs through continuously.

Who This Is For

We work with factories, in-house maintenance teams, EPC contractors and workshops. Requirements fall into four buying situations.

  • Planned maintenance. Scheduled shutdowns and preventive work where the part list is known and the priority is the full, correct set of items in one place.
  • Breakdown spares. Unplanned failures needing a specific bearing, fastener, sealant, lubricant or tool — usually urgent, often defined by a part number rather than a brand.
  • Workshop consumables. The recurring consumption of a workshop or store — tools, abrasives, fasteners, adhesives and lubricants — bought again and again on a routine cycle.
  • Site equipment. Ladders, scaffolding, paints and coatings for EPC and project work, where items must match the site's safety and specification requirements.

Brands We Source Through

We source through established brands such as Bosch, Stanley, Taparia, SKF, Loctite, Castrol, 3M Industrial, Makita and DeWalt, along with others suited to the specification in hand.

The advantage is multi-brand sourcing to specification. A single-brand policy often forces a maintenance team into a substitute that does not quite fit, or a premium item where a standard one would do. Sourcing across brands lets us work to the specification or part number you give us and place the item with the brand that matches it.

How We Handle Maintenance Requirements

  • Recurring MRO consumption. For items consumed continuously, the aim is a steady, repeatable route to supply rather than a fresh search each time.
  • Breakdown urgency. When a failure stops work, the requirement is routed through the same single point of contact so it does not get lost between vendors. We work the supplier network to locate the item; we do not claim guaranteed stock or fixed delivery times.
  • Specification and part-number matching. Send a part number, specification, drawing reference or sample; we source to it rather than to a brand label.
  • Consolidating low-value, high-frequency items. A full maintenance list is placed as one requirement through one contact, instead of separate orders for each small item.
  • Alternate brand equivalents. Where the exact brand is not essential, we can offer a comparable item from another brand to suit the application.

All supply runs on 100% GST-compliant billing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you supply against a part number or specification rather than a brand name?

Yes. A part number, specification, drawing reference or sample is enough to work from. We match the item to that requirement and can propose an alternate brand equivalent.

Do you handle small, recurring orders, or only large projects?

Both. Consolidating many low-value, high-frequency items is normal in this category, and routine replenishment is as relevant to us as a large project list.

Can I combine items from different categories in one requirement?

Yes. A maintenance list mixing tools, fasteners, lubricants, bearings and consumables can be placed through one contact rather than as separate orders.

What happens when we have an urgent breakdown requirement?

It is routed through your single point of contact and worked through the supplier network. We will tell you honestly what can be sourced; we do not promise delivery times or guaranteed stock.

How is billing handled?

All supply is on 100% GST-compliant billing, so procurement and accounts receive clean, consistent paperwork across the items supplied.
