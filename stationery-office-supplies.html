Stationery & Office Supplies for Corporates and Institutions

Stationery is the lowest-value and highest-frequency spend in most offices, and it is usually spread across too many vendors, too many small orders and too many GST invoices. InfiSource Corporation Private Limited sources and supplies everyday paper, writing, filing and desk essentials to corporates, industries, institutions and commercial establishments across India. For the procurement head, SCM manager, admin head or office manager, the outcome is one accountable partner for the whole stationery list rather than a fresh vendor for every item.

What We Source

We source the full working range an office, campus or print room consumes, grouped here by how it is actually used.

Paper and printing. A4 copier paper for daily printing and photocopying, and printer consumables for the machines in use. Paper is matched to the GSM and brand you specify, because the same office often runs different grades for general printing, bulk copying and quality documents.

Writing. Pens and markers across the range an office actually hands out, from everyday ball pens to permanent and correction markers. Board markers and dusters sit here too, as the consumable side of every meeting room.

Filing and organisation. Notebooks and registers, files and folders, envelopes and courier bags, and sticky notes and labels. These are the items that keep records, dispatch and paperwork moving, and they are typically bought in many small quantities rather than a few large ones.

Desk and meeting room. Staplers and punches, desk organisers, whiteboards, and stamps and ink pads. These are the shared, long-life items that still need replacing on a schedule and are easy to overlook until they run out.

Who This Is For

Corporate offices buy on a recurring monthly indent — the same paper, pens, files and consumables month after month, with one-off additions when a team moves floors or a project starts.

Schools and colleges buy in larger, seasonal blocks around admission and examination cycles, and need notebooks, registers, stationery for staff and paper in volumes that shift through the year.

Government offices buy against specifications and require clean, compliant paperwork and bills, where matching the stated make and grade matters as much as the price.

Print and DTP centres are driven by paper and printer consumables above all, and need consistent GSM and compatible consumables so output quality does not drift between batches.

Brands We Source Through

We source through established Indian stationery brands, including JK Paper, ITC, Cello, Luxor, Reynolds, Camlin, Kokuyo, Faber-Castell and Classmate. Sourcing multi-brand rather than single-brand is a deliberate advantage: your specification and your budget decide the make, not a fixed tie-up. Where a particular brand is preferred by a department, that preference can be honoured; where a grade is needed for a specific job, the brand that meets it can be selected. It also means the stationery list can be consolidated with one supplier even though it spans several manufacturers.

How We Handle Stationery Requirements

Stationery is managed differently from project or capital purchases, and we treat it that way.

  • Recurring monthly indents. We take your standing indent and the items that repeat, so the same list is not rebuilt and re-quoted each time.
  • Consumption-based ordering. Where you track usage, ordering can follow consumption rather than guesswork, which keeps fast-moving items available and slow-moving ones from piling up.
  • Consolidating many small SKUs. A stationery indent can run to dozens of low-value line items. We consolidate them into a single requirement rather than many separate small purchases.
  • Matching paper GSM and brand preferences. Paper is sourced to the GSM and make you specify, so print and copy output stays consistent.
  • Printer consumable compatibility. Consumables are matched to the printer models you run, so the right cartridge or toner is supplied for the machine in front of you.

Why InfiSource

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you handle our full stationery list, or only paper?

The full list — paper and printing, writing, filing and organisation, and desk and meeting-room items are all handled as one requirement.

Can we keep our existing brand preferences?

Yes. Tell us the makes and grades you want and we source to that specification. Where a preference is not the best fit for a particular job, we will say so and offer the alternative.

How do we order — do we have to give a large single order?

No. Most stationery is bought on a recurring monthly indent or against consumption. Consolidating many small SKUs makes frequent, low-value ordering manageable; it does not force it into one large order.

Can you match the paper GSM and printer consumables we already use?

Yes. Paper is sourced to the GSM and brand you specify, and consumables are matched to the printer models you run.

Is billing GST-compliant?

Yes. All billing is 100% GST-compliant, which matters when a stationery spend is spread across many small invoices.
