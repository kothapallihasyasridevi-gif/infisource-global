What We Supply in Hyderabad

We consolidate recurring and project-based purchasing across 18 sourcing categories. For a Hyderabad buyer, they usually fall into a few practical families.

Facilities and engineering: MEP solutions, facility maintenance, HVAC and air quality, safety and fire protection, and security and access control. These cover the building services that keep a campus or plant running, from electrical, mechanical and plumbing works to maintenance support, air handling, fire systems and access control.

Workplace and office: stationery and office supplies, office furniture, office appliances, IT and networking, and AV and meeting rooms. This is the day-to-day and fit-out spend of an office, covering consumables, desks and seating, appliances, network hardware and meeting-room equipment.

People and premises: housekeeping, pantry and cafeteria, gifting and uniforms, plants and landscaping, and recreation and engagement. These support your workforce and the environment that staff and visitors see.

Operational and specialist: printing and branding, packaging and storage, and sustainability. This covers branded material, packaging and storage supplies, and sustainability-linked requirements.

Every category is multi-brand and sourced to your specification, with 100% GST-compliant billing.
