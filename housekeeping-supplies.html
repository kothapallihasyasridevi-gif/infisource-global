Housekeeping Supplies for Corporate Offices and Facilities

Housekeeping supplies are a recurring, high-volume and high-SKU procurement line for every facility team. The buyer — a procurement head, SCM manager, admin head or facility manager — is usually chasing dozens of small items from several local vendors, reconciling multiple invoices and still running short at month-end. InfiSource Corporation Private Limited consolidates the complete facility hygiene and washroom supplies range into a single accountable sourcing arrangement, so the same cleaning chemicals, tissue, washroom consumables and housekeeping tools reach your site through one point of contact and one GST-compliant billing cycle.

What We Source

We source the full housekeeping consumables range to your specification, not to a fixed catalogue. The items below are grouped by how they are used on site.

Cleaning chemicals. Floor cleaners, surface cleaners, disinfectants, glass cleaners and toilet-cleaning chemicals form the bulk of most housekeeping budgets. We source them across concentrate and ready-to-use formats so you can match the chemical to the surface and the dilution practice your team already follows. (Lizol, Harpic, Dettol, Diversey, Taski and Buzil are among the brands sourced.)

Tissue, toilet rolls and paper products. Tissue papers, toilet rolls, paper towels and napkins are the fastest-moving consumables in any washroom. Because consumption is steady and predictable, this group is best handled as a scheduled recurring supply rather than a one-off order.

Washroom consumables and hygiene items. Hand wash, sanitisers, air fresheners and other washroom consumables keep restrooms presentable and compliant through the working day. These are typically ordered alongside the paper range so that a washroom is restocked as a single kit, not item by item.

Garbage bins, bags and liners. Garbage bins and the matching garbage bags and liners need to be sized to the dustbin already in use across the building. We source bins and liners together so that capacity, colour coding and fit are consistent floor to floor.

Mops, buckets, brushes and squeegees. Mops, buckets, brushes, squeegees and the Scotch-Brite range of scrubbers and cleaning pads are the reusable tools of the housekeeping team. They wear out at a different pace from consumables and are usually planned on a separate replenishment cycle.

Who This Is For

This category page is built around the buying situations we see across the facilities we support.

  • Corporate offices. Admin and facility teams maintain washrooms, pantry areas, workstations and common areas daily. The requirement is steady monthly consumables plus periodic replacement of cleaning tools and bins.
  • Hospitals. Hygiene standards are higher, disinfection is frequent and chemical selection matters. Consumption is heavy and specification-driven, so consistency of brand and formulation across the order is important.
  • Hotels. Guest-facing presentation drives the requirement — air fresheners, tissue, toilet rolls, napkins and washroom consumables must always be in stock and always presentable.
  • Malls and retail chains. Multi-location chains need the same item list and the same specification repeated across every outlet, which is easier to control through one sourcing partner than through separate local vendors per site.

Brands We Source Through

We are a sourcing and supply partner, not a manufacturer, and we work multi-brand by design. For housekeeping supplies that means we can source through 3M, Diversey, Taski, Godrej Professional, Lizol, Harpic, Dettol, Buzil and Scotch-Brite, among others, according to what your specification calls for.

The practical advantage is specification matching. If your facility standard names a particular formulation, dilution ratio or brand for a given surface, we source to that standard rather than substituting whatever a single vendor happens to stock. Where you are open to alternatives, a multi-brand network lets us match the requirement on specification instead of being locked to one manufacturer's range.

How We Handle Housekeeping Requirements

Housekeeping is not a project purchase; it is a consumption pattern, and we treat it that way.

  • Recurring monthly supply. Because tissue, toilet rolls, chemicals and washroom consumables are consumed steadily, we plan them as a recurring supply cycle tied to your consumption rather than as isolated orders.
  • Consumption patterns. We work from your historical usage and site count to understand how much is consumed per floor, per washroom or per outlet, so replenishment reflects real demand.
  • Consolidation of many SKUs. A single facility typically buys dozens of small housekeeping SKUs. Consolidating them through one supplier reduces the number of vendors, invoices and follow-ups your team manages.
  • Specification matching. For each line item we match brand, pack size, formulation and fit to your written requirement before sourcing.
  • One point of contact. Your requirement, queries and follow-ups run through a single accountable contact across the category.
