Pantry & Cafeteria Supplies for Offices and Campuses

For an admin head, facilities manager or procurement manager, the office pantry is a small operation that behaves like a large one: it never stops, it runs across many small-value items, and a single missing item — tea, cups, water — is noticed the same morning. InfiSource Corporation Private Limited sources pantry and cafeteria essentials for corporates, IT parks, hospitals, educational campuses and commercial establishments across India, so you deal with one accountable partner instead of a long list of vendors.

What We Source

Our range is grouped around how a pantry is actually stocked, so you can order across the whole list or just the groups you need.

Beverages. Tea and coffee, vending supplies, beverages and juices, and dairy whitener and milk form the daily consumption core of most pantries. We source these in the formats your staff and vending machines use, covering the morning and afternoon beverage rounds from one place.

Snacks and biscuits. Biscuits and snacks, the Britannia range, the Parle range, and soups and instant noodles cover the between-meetings and late-shift demand that builds quietly through the day.

Disposables and cutlery. Disposable cups and cutlery are consumed at high volume and are the items most likely to run short. We keep these alongside the consumables they are used with, so replenishment is planned rather than reactive.

Water and dairy. Drinking water, and sugar and condiments sit with the beverages they support. Packaged drinking water for meeting rooms and floors is among the most frequent pantry requirements, handled as a recurring line rather than a one-off purchase.

Crockery and serving ware. Crockery and serving ware for cafeterias, serveries and guest areas complete the category — the durable items that need to match what is already in use.

Who This Is For

This category suits any establishment that feeds people on site, whether staff, students, patients or visitors.

  • Corporate pantries and offices — daily tea, coffee, biscuits and water for staff floors, with replenishment that follows headcount and meeting-room usage.
  • IT parks and multi-floor campuses — high-frequency consumption across several floors and cafeterias, where consistent stock and standardised items matter.
  • Hospitals — pantry and cafeteria supplies for staff, visitors and canteen services, consumed around the clock and across shifts.
  • Educational campuses — hostels, canteens, staff rooms and event days, where consumption rises during term time.
  • Commercial establishments — reception, guest areas and staff rooms where a well-stocked pantry is part of the visitor experience.

Brands We Source Through

We source through established brands, including Tata Tea, Nescafe, Bru, Bisleri, Kinley, Paper Boat, ITC Foods, Britannia and Parle. Sourcing across multiple brands matters in a pantry because your requirement is rarely a single product: you may want one brand of tea and another of coffee, or a mix of biscuit and snack brands across floors, or brands your staff or guests already expect. Multi-brand sourcing lets you specify by brand, pack size and variant rather than accepting whatever a single vendor carries. InfiSource is a sourcing and supply partner — we source through a verified supplier network and do not manufacture any of these products.

How We Handle Pantry Requirements

Pantry supply is a consumption cycle, not a project, and we handle it that way.

  • Recurring consumption cycles. Pantry items are consumed continuously and predictably. We work to your consumption pattern so ordering becomes routine rather than a scramble.
  • High-frequency replenishment. Tea, coffee, water, cups and biscuits are replenished often. We consolidate these frequent lines into a managed requirement instead of daily small orders.
  • Freshness and shelf life. Beverages, dairy whitener, biscuits and snacks have shelf lives that need watching. We help you stock to realistic consumption so items move while fresh.
  • Vending machine consumables. For sites running tea and coffee vending, we source the consumables the machines need alongside the pantry lines, so the vending point is not a separate buying exercise.
  • Consolidating many small-value SKUs. A pantry list is long and each line is low value. Consolidating through one partner reduces the invoices, follow-ups and deliveries your team manages.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we order across the full pantry list, or do we need to split it between vendors?

You can order across the full range. Tea, coffee, water, biscuits, disposables, dairy and crockery can all be sourced through InfiSource, which is the point of consolidating the category.

Do you supply for vending machines as well as manual pantries?

Yes. Vending supplies are part of the range, alongside the manual pantry items, so both can be handled together.

We have several floors or sites with different needs. Can that be managed together?

Yes. Multi-location requirements can be coordinated through one point of contact, with items specified per site where needs differ.

How do we handle items that are consumed very quickly, such as water and disposable cups?

These are treated as high-frequency replenishment lines rather than occasional purchases, so they are built into your regular requirement.

Is InfiSource a manufacturer or a supplier?

InfiSource is a sourcing and supply partner. We source through a verified supplier network and do not manufacture the products.
